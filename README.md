# Professions and Skills
A Minecraft mod (NeoForge 1.21.1): skill trees that level from what you do, forging and the other crafting professions at a Guild Ledger workshop, and D&D-style races, classes and ability scores. Part of the [Dungeon Delving](https://github.com/Dunedain-Ranger-Dev/dungeon-delving) modpack.

It works on its own, and links up with these when they're installed: Pufferfish's Skills (the skill trees), Iron's Spells and Spell Engine (magic schools, forged staves), Better Combat, Apotheosis, Curios and Patchouli (the Smith's Handbook). With Dungeon Delving, mobs in its dungeons have its levels, and its dungeon metals fill the tier metal tags (`ddprofessions:tier_metals/tier_1` to `tier_4`) that the higher workshop stations are made from.
